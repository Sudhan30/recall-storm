# Recall Storm Architecture

Recall Storm is a deterministic retail regulatory and product recall execution platform organized into 22 domain packages:

1. `recallstorm.core`: Deterministic IDs (`ids.py`), error hierarchy (`errors.py`), configuration (`config.py`), JSONL logging and PII redaction (`logging_setup.py`), timezone-aware and local wall-clock utilities (`clock.py`), JSONL schema validation (`jsonl.py`), entity validation (`validate.py`), and module provenance registry (`registry.py`).
2. `recallstorm.ingest`: Data parsers for USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts, openFDA enforcement reports, CPSC recalls, vendor catalogs, OCR labels, store CSVs, planograms, and staffing rosters.
3. `recallstorm.aliases`: Multilingual additive translations, character span tracking, OCR error normalization, E-number/CI-number lexicons, decoy rejection (`Red 40`, `D&C Red 33`, `Red Pepper`, `3 Pepper`), unclear label detection, and multi-item kit/bundle splitting.
4. `recallstorm.rules`: Statutory and regulatory rule engine implementing `evaluate_sku(sku, rules, state, at, classifier=None) -> SkuDecision` for FD&C Red No. 3 (CA AB 418 and FDA food/ingested drug revocations), Brominated Vegetable Oil (BVO), Potassium Bromate, Propylparaben, Minnesota PFAS Statute 116.943, and CARB multi-purpose lubricant VOC limits.
5. `recallstorm.adjudicate`: Human overrides, stratified QA sampling, review queues, heuristic/Gemini classifiers, evidence cards, and audit trails.
6. `recallstorm.gs1`: GS1 Mod-10 check digits, GTIN-8/12/13/14 normalization, Application Identifiers (`01`, `10`, `11`, `17`, `21`), GS1 Digital Link URIs, EPCIS movement events, and lot-range matching.
7. `recallstorm.store`: Store layout geometry, aisle ordering, shelf facings, backroom staging, signage flashing, free-text `StoreNote` exception classification, and `StoreFormat` profiles.
8. `recallstorm.labor`: Associate shift calendars, skills, task duration estimates, district floater pools (enforcing one store per floater window), and greedy/balanced task assignment.
9. `recallstorm.allocate`: DC stock ledgers, store priorities, substitute demand calculation, and proportional/priority allocation implementing `allocate_substitutes(demand, stock, stores) -> list[Allocation]`.
10. `recallstorm.plan`: Shelf task builders, exception planners, store playbooks, handoffs, deadline guards, human-readable explanations, and `plan.jsonl` assembly.
11. `recallstorm.sim`: Discrete-event simulation (`MOVE`, `PULL`, `PLACE`, `DONE`), walking path models, operational disruptions, and completion curves.
12. `recallstorm.scenario_gs1`: Lot-specific recall scenarios driven by openFDA enforcement notices and GS1 lot codes.
13. `recallstorm.states`: State-specific compliance modules across NY, IL, PA, WA, NJ, MN, TX, LA, WV, UT, AZ, CA, ME, and VT.
14. `recallstorm.recall_lots`: FDA/FSIS notice parsers, wildcard/Julian lot matchers, Best-By windows, consignee distribution lists, recall depth policies, effectiveness checks, SSCC-18 serial traceability, catch-weight lots, BOM cascades, and mock recall generators.
15. `recallstorm.food_safety`: Cold-chain TCS holds, allergen cross-contact, SSOP sanitation logs, consumer illness complaints, ICMSF sampling, HACCP deviations, foreign material 7mm rules, boil-water advisories, produce antimicrobial wash logs, FIFO date audits, pest holds, and salvage reconditioning.
16. `recallstorm.inventory`: Perpetual reconciliation, GL shrink accounting, emergency cycle counts, locked quarantine cages, RMAs, FEFO lot subledgers, in-transit ASN interception, e-commerce dark stores, SBT consignment stock, demo station holds, cross-dock interception, and witnessed compactor destruction.
17. `recallstorm.pricing`: POS hard stop-sale locks, scale PLU deactivation, cashier lot prompts, circular ad conflicts, CVP markdown lockouts, substitute price parity, ESL LED flashing, no-receipt recall refunds, WIC/EBT APL hotfixes, cryptographic price-file governance, CRV bottle deposit credits, and GS1 AI(8112) coupon halts.
18. `recallstorm.comms`: Store action bulletins, 5-minute shift huddles, loyalty household notifications, supplier quarantine directives, FDA status filings, 14-day shelf placards, contact center triage scripts, PR crisis statements, pharmacy NDC outreach, foodservice advisories, union hazmat notices, and multilingual localization.
19. `recallstorm.vendor`: Recall chargeback invoicing, additive reformulation tracking, ISO 17025 COA validation, DSD driver DEX coordination, credit memo reconciliation, product liability COI tenders, private-label QA, supplier scorecards, item onboarding gates, FDA PREDICT import holds, co-packer plant suffix decoding, and chargeback dispute arbitration.
20. `recallstorm.dc_ops`: Inbound receiving RF blocks, forward pick-face freezes, outbound wave interception, YMS glad-hand trailer locks, EDI 204/990 reverse load tendering, reclamation sortation chutes, LPN genealogy, surge quarantine slotting, -10F freezer holds, AS/RS and bot inhibits, and RCRA hazmat segregation.
21. `recallstorm.analytics` & `recallstorm.governance`: Drill SLA scorecards, store risk profiles, depletion forecasting, pencil-whipping anomaly detection, longitudinal trends, root-cause text mining, labor motion efficiency, Total Cost of Recall (TCOR) bridges, shelf void capture rates, supplier benchmarking, digital twin calibration, C-suite war room digests, SHA-256 Merkle audit binders, dual-control approvals, sworn manager affidavits, 7-year FSMA WORM retention, RBAC/SIEM logs, SOP Git versioning, statutory calendars, anonymous whistleblower intake, third-party Steritech/Ecolab audits, LMS certifications, customer PII tokenization, and Board Risk Committee reporting.
22. `recallstorm.route` & `recallstorm.cli`: First-fit-decreasing return truck packing implementing `pack_returns(pulled_cases, skus, trucks, stores) -> list[RouteManifest]` and the `python -m recallstorm` CLI.
